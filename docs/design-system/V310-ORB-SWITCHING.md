# V3.10 · Linked interactive orb transitions
After fixing the missing bundle in V3.8 and swallowed close gestures in V3.9, tests uncovered a related race: the original card's CSS class switched to collapsed at the beginning of the 920ms closing morph. Users could now tap another orb while the engine remained busy, and this click was ignored. The new `pendingOpen` intent is captured during closing, then its corresponding expansion starts immediately after the reverse animation completes. Escape can cancel the pending switch; navigation clears pending intents. This preserves fluid continuity instead of forcing repeated taps.
