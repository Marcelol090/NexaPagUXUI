# NexaPag V3.18 · Expressive Bot-Orb Identity
Three original character silhouettes grounded in the Grok Bot UI language of a consistent family, differentiated shapes and restrained expressive states. No assets or code from xAI are copied.

| Financial meaning | Shape | Materials | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entradas | Live droplet/pebble | mint glass | slower asymmetric breathing, attentive eyes |
| Saídas | Soft triangular bot | coral glass | brisk triangular inhale and focused blink |
| Estornos | Six-sided squircle | violet glass | long calm settling and soft blink |

Each orb keeps its numeric amount and category text. The SVG-style outline is a 48-point clip polygon generated locally. WAAPI animates a seamless loop in idle with the first and final contours identical, respecting reduced motion and document visibility. On opening and closing, the existing shared-element liquid ghost interpolates from or into that same distinctive contour rather than popping from a circular placeholder. Identity dissolves before the orb expands into the card; other card navigation remains intact.
QA: different shape IDs and contour strings, two eyes and visible amount each, reduced-motion static, morph starting geometry matches resting contour, reinitialization when the tab is re-rendered.
